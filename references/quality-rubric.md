# Quality rubric: score each 1–5
1. Brand distinctiveness (could this be used for an unrelated company unchanged?).
2. Relevance to client, audience and product.
3. Image credibility, permissions and factual honesty.
4. Copy specificity, clarity and evidence.
5. UX usability, mobile, accessible contrast, keyboard, motion reduction, performance.
6. Consistency of system across typography, materials, images, layouts.

1 = obstructive/unsupported; 2 = major issues; 3 = serviceable but generic; 4 = coherent with minor issues; 5 = distinctive, grounded, functional. Never claim overall score is an objective scientific measure.

## Gates
- **Stop:** invented real person/product/testimonial, rights-unclear image as real, unreadable mobile, critical CTA broken, no chosen art direction.
- **Fix before shipping:** missing source photo, unchecked Cyrillic font, captions detached from imagery, generative text artifacts, cropped hero, WCAG contrast failures.
- **Audit:** photo hands/text/screens/reflections, legibility at actual icon size, screen reader structure, form labels and success/error states.

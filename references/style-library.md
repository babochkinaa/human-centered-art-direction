# Nine visual directions — lenses, not templates
Select based on the actual brand, not based on popularity. Each requires its own typographic and visual tokens.

1. **Editorial Design** — strong narrative hierarchy, literary or news-magazine pacing, expressive typography, commissioned or authentic photography, meaningful pull quotes. Risk: newspaper clichés, too much delicate hairline decoration.
2. **Documentary Realism** — candid real people, authentic settings and unposed work, specific objects, daylight or practical light, evidence-based captions. Never fabricate a real documentary scene. Risk: fake imperfections and voyeurism.
3. **Swiss Modernism** — asymmetrical disciplined grid, informative sans typography, purposeful spacing, objective visuals, high contrast. Risk: sterile generic UI; invented numbers and micro-labels.
4. **Neo-Zine** — editorial collage, handmade cutouts, scans, photocopy marks, expressive scale, zine rhythm. Risk: compromised readability, unlicensed scans, artificial chaos.
5. **Tactile Minimalism** — few elements, honest material surfaces (paper, ink, fabric, stone) that carry meaning, controlled warm/cool contrasts. Risk: indiscriminate grain and beige sameness.
6. **Refined Brutalism** — striking hierarchy, unapologetic grids, raw functional elements, powerful type, disciplined restraint. Risk: hostile contrast/UX, all-caps overload.
7. **Human Sketch** — annotated drafts, imperfect original pen strokes, workshop notes, expressive authorship. Risk: fabricated handwritten personal documents or illegible UI.
8. **Analog Digital** — analog photo sequences, contact sheets, scan quality, precise digital layers; process visible but trustworthy. Risk: meaningless nostalgic overlays.
9. **Contemporary Art Direction** — concept-driven signature visual language: unusual crops, original graphical grammar, purposeful photography and materials. Risk: originality theater, inconsistent gimmicks.

## Pairing & decision
Offer three **nontrivial** concepts, such as Editorial/Documentary, Swiss/Art-direction, Neo-Zine/Analog. For each articulate specific distinctive point of view and downstream production costs. Resist "all nine at once." No single direction is automatically better for AI education.

## Trend references (not mandatory style prescriptions)
Canva 2026: Explorecore, Opt-Out Era, Texture Check, Notes App Chic, Zinegeist. Adobe 2026: Connectioneering, Local Flavor, All the Feels; Webflow: ownable visual systems; Figma Config 2026: dynamic glyphs + structured composition. The final language must be **original to the client's subject**.

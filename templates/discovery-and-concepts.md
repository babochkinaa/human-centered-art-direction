# Interaction template
## Brief capture (only missing essentials)
1. What should this design accomplish, for whom and where will it appear?
2. What exists already: identity, site, references, examples you like/dislike?
3. What real photo/video/product screenshots and verified facts can you provide?
4. Any legal, language, accessibility, platform or brand restrictions?
If the user gave these, don't ask again.

## Concept presentation (before creation)
For each of three directions: concept name, one-line thesis, hero/key image, visual rhythm/layout, 4–6 hex tokens, two candidate font families with Cyrillic verification pending, photographic shot list and gaps, material/textural vocabulary, UI copy tone, 2 things to avoid, best use-case, production risk. Close with a direct request to choose one. Never render a finished site before choice.

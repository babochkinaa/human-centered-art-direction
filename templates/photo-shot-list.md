# Real-photo request
Ask for prioritized, real, rights-cleared assets, not everything indiscriminately.

| Priority | Subject | Scene / action | Format and crop | Placement | Consent/provenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | actual product/person/work | unposed relevant action | landscape 16:9 + portrait 4:5 if possible, highest original resolution | hero | permission required |
| High | process/space | real working detail | 4:3 or 3:2 | section | permission required |
| Medium | documents/interface | genuine screen with private data redacted | lossless screenshot | evidence | no confidential data |

Do not ask for identifiable minor or client pictures when not necessary. Never modify real features, credentials, awards or outcomes without user consent. If unavailable: typography-first or abstract/illustrative concept, labeled as illustrative.
